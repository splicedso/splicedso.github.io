Five formats across four model sizes. A weight in a 0.5B model costs up to 1.6 times one in a 7B; Q4_0 is cheapest at every width; the Q5_K_M against Q6_K order flips with width.

The cost of unpacking

Each format's matrix-vector kernel measured alone. Q4_0 and Q4_K read identical bytes at identical speed; Q4_K's unpacking costs 40% more chip energy. Explains note 2's largest residual and corrects its roof.

R2 Open questions

What we want to know next.

Width or depth?

Small models pay up to 1.6 times more per weight, but the models that show it differ in width, depth and vocabulary at once. Separating them says which fixed cost to attack.

Why so little data?

Two servers in the latest MLPerf rounds report LLM energy at the wall. A reproducible way to measure serving energy under realistic load would be worth more than another speed record.

One setting everywhere?

On this Mac the best draft threshold lies between 0.5 and 0.8, and lower is worse. Whether the same setting holds on an NVIDIA GPU and for other model pairs is the next test.
