R2 Open questions

What we want to know next.

Width or depth?

Small models pay up to 1.6 times more per weight, but the models that show it differ in width, depth and vocabulary at once. Separating them says which fixed cost to attack.

Does it hold at scale?

Tokens per megawatt-hour for datacenter GPUs, from a roofline and published specs, checked against MLPerf results.

Joules or seconds?

Speculative decoding is sold on latency. On memory-bound hardware it might cost energy instead of saving it.
