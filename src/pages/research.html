R2 Open questions

What we want to know next.

Width or depth?

Small models pay up to 1.6 times more per weight, but the models that show it differ in width, depth and vocabulary at once. Separating them says which fixed cost to attack.

Why so little data?

Two servers in the latest MLPerf rounds report LLM energy at the wall. A reproducible way to measure serving energy under realistic load would be worth more than another speed record.

Below 0.5?

A 0.5 draft threshold was the best tested, and the lowest. Sweeping lower, and across model pairs and machines, would say whether there is one setting to recommend.
