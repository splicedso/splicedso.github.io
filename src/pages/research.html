Is it the kernel?

The energy model leaves Q4_0 29% cheaper and Q5_K_M 19% dearer than their bytes predict. Measuring each unpacking kernel alone should account for it.

Tokens per megawatt-hour for datacenter GPUs, from a roofline and published specs, checked against MLPerf results.
