J1 The lab

The engineering side of the AI build-out, measured.

Spliced is a small research lab. We study what it costs to run a model, in watts and joules as well as seconds, from one laptop up to how inference clusters are designed.

Every result ships with its method, its code and its raw data, and every note says what it cannot show.

A1

Inference engineering

Where decode time and energy go: memory bandwidth, kernels, quantization formats, batching, the KV cache, serving at cluster scale.

 A2

Data science

Measurement you can trust: repeated runs, held-out validation, and models whose coefficients have to make physical sense.

 A3

Software engineering

The tools that make the measurements repeatable, open source, small enough to read in one sitting.
J2 Findings so far
TP1 / TP2, F16 vs Q4_0
2.1×

what full precision really costs over 4-bit at the board. Measured at the chip alone, the two look the same: the memory energy is invisible there.

batch 1 → 8, Q4_0
72%

less energy per token from serving eight requests at once. Batch 2 saves 1%: the GPU reaches its power ceiling at the second sequence.

Q4_K / Q4_0, same bytes
40%

more chip energy for the same traffic at the same speed. The two formats read identical bytes; the difference is entirely how the bits are unpacked.
