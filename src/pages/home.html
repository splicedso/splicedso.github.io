What a token costs.

We measure the energy AI spends on every token and every correct answer, on hardware people own, and publish what we find.

Measured, not estimated.

Spliced is a small research group. We run open models on an Apple M5 Pro, an AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395 and an RTX 5070 Ti Laptop GPU, read each chip's own power sensors while it works, and report the energy of every token and of every correct answer. Every run is repeated, warmed up, and published with its raw data.

{{note_count}} notes so far

Each note asks one question: what a quantization format costs, when batching pays, whether speculative decoding saves energy or only time. The prediction is written down before the run and stays in the record when it turns out wrong.

Two model families, both early

Trace is a draft model trained to predict a larger one, for speculative decoding. Lattice is a model kept accurate below 4 bits per weight. Each has a first version and a measured result. Neither has cleared its bar yet, though calibrated 2-bit already beats 4-bit per correct answer on a 32B model. The Models page says exactly where they stand.

Two tools

Phase is the open-source power meter behind every number here. Foundry is a desktop app, still in development, that runs a model on your own machine and shows the speed and energy of each answer.
