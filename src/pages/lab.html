Lab

Open research.

We study what it takes to run AI well, and how to make it cheaper.

Every answer has a price in joules.

Benchmarks report speed. We report what speed costs: the energy a chip spends on each token, and on each correct answer.

We measure on hardware people own. An Apple M5 Pro, an AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395, an RTX 5070 Ti Laptop GPU. Each run is repeated, warmed up, and published with its raw data.

Small models, one job each

Trace drafts tokens so a large model can accept several at once. Lattice keeps answers intact at 2 bits. Neither has beaten the stock model on our bar yet.

A model ships only when it beats the best open model on the same machine, per correct answer.

Everything open

Every note states its prediction before the run, and keeps it when it is wrong. Code, data and limits are public.
