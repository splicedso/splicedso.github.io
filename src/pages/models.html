Models

Small models. One job each.

Two families. Each has one target number, and a box that says where it stands. The bar they have to beat is the measured recipe for running a stock model. A third comes when these two clear theirs.

Trace

Draft models for one target.

A small model that predicts a large one, so speculative decoding accepts more of its guesses.

v0
0.5B, distilled from Qwen2.5 7B. +3% accepted tokens.
v1
Trained on the 7B's probabilities. At 35,000 prompts: +1.4 points agreement, no faster than the stock draft.
Bar
+3 points top-1 agreement over the stock draft.
Now
Paused. On-policy training did not help, and at its best threshold every draft, trained or stock, serves within 1%. The stock draft ships.

Lattice

Answers that survive 2 bits.

Models that keep their accuracy when compressed below 4 bits per weight.

v0
Calibrated Q2_K. GSM8K 21% → 42% at the same size.
32B
Calibrated Q2_K beats Q4_0 per correct answer by 16% at equal accuracy. At 7B it still loses by 21%.
v1
Quantization-aware fine-tuning of the 1.5B onto a 2-bit grid. Predicted 52%, short of the bar.
Bar
At least 60% at Q2_K size, cheaper per answer than Q4_0 on an M5 Pro.
Now
Training has started and shares the GPU with Trace; it resumes when the harvest ends. Then it is measured on both machines, against a control that separates shorter answers from fewer bits.
