D Data

Every measurement, in one table.

The Spliced dataset holds every decode-energy figure behind our research notes: two laptops, two runtimes, four model sizes, eight number formats, batch sizes 1 to 8, and the audited datacenter servers from MLPerf. Nothing in it is interpolated or fitted. Each row names the note it comes from.
