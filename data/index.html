Data

Every measurement, in one table.

Every energy figure behind our research. Measured, never fitted, each row traced to its note.

Download the CSV ↓

Every number, with its source.

Every row is one model, in one format, on one machine. Rows from our own machines are measured by Phase, twice, with the second pass reversed. The datacenter rows come from published MLPerf results, and their notes say so. Nothing is estimated from a spec sheet.

Chip and whole machine

Chip energy comes from the processor's own power sensors. Whole-machine energy comes from the battery or the wall. Both are listed where we have them.

Per token and per answer

Energy per token says what a format costs. Energy per correct answer says whether it was worth it. The second table is the one to read first.
